# Third-party data and attribution

AIIO Canada's original software and documentation are licensed under the
Apache License 2.0 in `LICENSE`. AIIO Canada combines original software and
research transformations with public
data published by Canadian governments, municipalities, system operators and
public agencies. Third-party data and source documents are not relicensed by
the Apache licence or by this repository.

The authoritative source, publisher, canonical URL, retrieval method, evidence
status and licence note for every registered source are recorded in
`data/registry/sources.json`. Reusers must follow the licence and attribution
requirements attached to each source and should cite the registered publisher
and source URL.

Raw source files are retained only where redistribution is supported by the
recorded terms. The Alberta Major Projects raw export is intentionally excluded
from Git until its redistribution terms are confirmed; its retrieval manifest
and content hash remain available for provenance and local reproducibility.

Model outputs, inferred classifications and scenarios are not representations
or endorsements by the original data publishers. Evidence status and
publication boundaries travel with the corresponding model-run artifacts.
