# Public website brief

## Product promise

In a few minutes, a visitor should understand what AI infrastructure is being proposed, where resource pressure may emerge, why the model produces that signal and which evidence or assumption supports it.

## Primary journeys

1. **Prepare a decision:** define a public project, see which current evidence and counterfactual pathways are relevant, and identify decision gates without inventing a cost forecast.
2. **Understand the issue:** switch to Research Mode for the framing question, Alberta baseline and evidence architecture.
3. **Inspect investment:** filter the AI project register by place, stage, period and evidence status.
4. **Monitor pressure:** compare labour, construction-cost, material and power signals without mixing units.
5. **Explore a scenario:** change investment quantum and timing; inspect ranges and paths while retaining counterfactual labels.
6. **Audit the result:** open methods, source records, model version and downloads.
7. **Keep current:** read the weekly evidence digest; reviewed evidence may update the register but never model parameters automatically.

## Planned routes

- `/` — Decision Mode project definition, decision boundary, evidence channels, graph paths and actions.
- `/research` — Research Mode observatory overview and current Alberta signal.
- `/evidence` — searchable sources, claims and AI project register.
- `/pressure` — labour, costs, materials and power dashboards.
- `/scenario` — scenario controls, results and path explanation.
- `/methods` — research design, graph ontology, limitations and validation.
- `/digest` — weekly evidence editions.
- `/downloads` — release manifests, CSV/JSON, workbook and citations.

## Interface rules

- Default to the Alberta pilot but show the Canada expansion path.
- Label observed, inferred, assumed and scenario values in place.
- Keep observed market screens, counterfactual scenario paths and calibration gaps in separate fields.
- Never present an AI-attributable project cost or schedule estimate before the reviewed translation layer is calibrated.
- Use plain-language summaries with expandable technical detail.
- Show units, price year, geography and period on every quantitative display.
- Preserve stable URLs for sources, releases, scenarios and digest editions.
- Meet WCAG 2.2 AA as the release target.

## Initial design direction

A serious working observatory rather than a marketing page: deep navy analytical surfaces, warm paper backgrounds, teal for evidence and model flow, amber for caution, compact metadata and generous space around primary findings.
