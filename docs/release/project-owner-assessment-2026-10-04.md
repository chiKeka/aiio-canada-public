# Project owner assessment

The assessment entry point connects project context, editable work packages, selected AI construction phases, shared-resource scheduling and remaining financial exposure. It reuses the resource-constrained paired scheduler rather than deriving a generic AI premium from total project budget.

Supported numerical use is a conditional owner-input scenario for Alberta schools, hospitals and government buildings. Other contexts remain qualitative. Four trade headings begin with unknown quantities, dates, resource pools and costs. Location alone does not establish shared crews. Published project envelopes do not supply phase paid hours or capacity.

Baseline, selected AI and mitigation cases share owner packages and competing private demand. The scheduler carries unmet hours and precedence through the horizon. Project float applies once and overhead is priced once at the project milestone. Fixed commitments do not reprice; unknown exposure, capacity or selected phase scope withholds a complete financial estimate. Baseline escalation is separate from the incremental comparison. Supplier and enabling-infrastructure links remain qualitative where amounts and incidence are unresolved.

The optional $50M school fixture, including $8M variable electrical exposure, is hypothetical. Every work quantity, capacity and pricing input in it is an assumption. No actual school budget or empirical AI cost attribution is asserted.

The phase catalogue retains release hashes and review qualifications. Independent empirical review remains pending. This change does not promote refresh candidates, change the deployed pilot, or authorize a merge or deployment.

Numerical contracts and portable desktop/mobile browser checks are included in quality CI. Current-input JSON and print exports are generated from the same assessment record. Draft retention and reset are verified with the browser harness.

The production-ready refinement leads with budget, entered exposure, contingency and paired result cards. Short status lines retain conditional-use, missing-input, verification-date and frozen-release qualifications. Detailed methodology and phase evidence are expandable, and exports retain the complete assessment record. The final browser harness verifies that refresh attempts do not advance the displayed frozen release date.
