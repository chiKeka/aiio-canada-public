# Research integrity and academic use

The Observatory is a public-data research instrument. Its code, observations and scenario outputs do not replace a researcher's original argument, source verification, ethics review or required AI disclosure.

The pilot uses public secondary sources. No participant interviews, surveys or restricted administrative data are authorized by this public snapshot. A later change of method requires the appropriate institutional ethics assessment before collection.

Users must verify sources, distinguish observations from assumptions, cite sources and disclose material AI assistance under their institution's rules. Scenario outputs remain uncalibrated structural diagnostics, not causal findings, forecasts or project-specific delay estimates. Independent empirical validation remains pending.
