## Preferred named comparator: South Windsong school, southwest Airdrie

The strongest dated public-owner record is Rocky View Schools' [June 3, 2025
media release](https://www.rockyview.ab.ca/download/513802), archived as
`public-windsong-board-release/release.pdf`. Page 1 identifies the new South
Windsong Kindergarten–Grade 9 school, completed design, EllisDon construction
contract award, expected construction start in spring 2025, and expected fall
2027 opening. The PDF's written month resolves the ambiguous numeric date on
[the corresponding board webpage](https://www.rockyview.ab.ca/archived-news/post/construction-of-new-kindergarten-grade-9-school-in-south-windsong-about-to-get-underway).
This is an award-known whole-school comparator, with procurement complete by
June 3, 2025; it is not evidence that its electrical/civil subcontracts remain
uncommitted.

The board's [current construction status
page](https://www.rockyview.ab.ca/about-rvs/building-our-student-spaces), archived
as `public-rvs-project-status/page.html`, corroborates spring 2025 construction
start, EllisDon award, expected 2027 opening, and site at 560 Osborne Drive SW.
The board does not label that page with an observation/update date; October 3 is
the retrieval date, not an invented progress-observation date.

The successful contractor's [March 31, 2025 financial-close
announcement](https://www.ellisdon.com/news/ellisdon-achieves-financial-close-on-alberta-s-p3-schools-bundle-5-project),
archived as `public-ellisdon-school-financial-close/page.html`, identifies Alberta
P3 Schools Bundle #5, design/build/finance/maintenance award and its Airdrie
school. This is contractor-primary corroboration of the bundle milestone, not
a public-owner electrical award record. It describes Airdrie as K–8 while the
board's later release describes K–9; preserve the naming revision and match by
site/project rather than treating the grade labels as separate projects.

## Alternatives and exclusions

[Highway 566 / QEII Balzac interchange, provincial project
2976](https://majorprojects.alberta.ca/details/Hwy-2-SH-566-Interchange-Upgrades-Balzac/2976),
archived as `public-balzac-project-register/page.html`, is close to the proposed
Rocky View/Balzac catchment. The October 3 retrieval displays 2026–2028 schedule,
$92.0 million estimated total project cost, Alberta Transportation and Economic
Corridors as developer, and AECOM as architect. Its stage combines under
construction with design. There is no retained tender closing date, awarded
contract value or detailed civil/electrical construction schedule. The estimate
is a project envelope, not a priced exposed package. Do not label that $92
million as an AI-induced road cost or an award. Earlier reported schedules and
amounts require their own dated primary records before reconciliation.

The board's current page places **Bayview K–9** at 1200 Bayview Gate SW, says
design is complete and a contractor is being finalized, references construction
approval in 2024, and targets 2028 opening. That undated page does not establish
an unawarded package as of October 3, 2026, an electrical/civil bid window, or a
2025–2027 delivery commitment. Keep it as an alternate acquisition lead.

The provincial [active school
projects](https://www.alberta.ca/active-school-projects) page, archived as
`public-alberta-schools-status/page.html`, corroborates construction funding for
Windsong/Bayview in Airdrie. Its capital-approval classifications do not establish
procurement award dates or prices.

[APC AB-2025-03975](https://purchasing.alberta.ca/posting/AB-2025-03975) is a
Schools Bundle #7 procurement discovery lead. The downloaded successful HTTP
response is only the JavaScript app shell; the retained bytes contain no
posting-specific bid terms. Mark `public-schools-bundle7-apc` as
`app_shell_not_verified`; do not cite its dates or link it to Bayview from a search
snippet. Alberta Open Government's school-bundle search/API returned HTTP 403
during this acquisition, so neither historical tender dates nor financial terms
were recovered there. No account or paid access was used.

## Next evidence needed before quantification

1. Owner/prime tender or award record identifying the named school site and
   electrical or civil package, with issue date, close date, award date and
   installation window. For the highway alternative, an actual procurement
   contract number and bid/award record are needed first.
2. Priced package quantities and remaining uncommitted amount, with the contract
   escalation/risk-transfer clauses. An aggregate school or road budget cannot
   substitute for exposed commitments.
3. The corresponding CAL3 Phase 1 trade/package calendar and labour requirements,
   plus evidence that both packages use the same relevant trade catchment during
   overlapping work periods. Keep capacity unknown until measured or explicitly
   scenario-assumed; shared region alone does not prove a shortage.

These are acquisition gaps, not evidence of absence. No contractor, school board
or government staff were contacted.
